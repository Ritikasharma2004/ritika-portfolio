Chicago Bike-Share Rebalancing

Aug 2026 · Python, SQL, Geospatial
  • Analysed 16,70,686 Divvy trips across three seasons, joined to every station's dock capacity, to answer where the rebalancing vans should go.
  • Found the obvious signal is useless — the median station nets zero bikes over a full day. Bikes move 6.3x more within a day than across one, so rebalancing is a timing problem, not a volume one.
